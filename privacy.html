PRIVACY POLICY

Roadster Rush · effective October 22, 2026

Roadster Rush does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your best score, coins, unlocked paints and settings are saved locally on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we only see the public leaderboard entries.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports. They contain no personal information.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
